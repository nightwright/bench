# Art-asset pipeline (landing pages / marketing visuals)

For greenfield landing pages or marketing visuals that need
generated art (the "amazing layouts" recipe from elaya's examples):

1. **Structure** — `landing-page-design` skill (installed user-level):
   intake → outline → copy → visual system. Hal writes the message.
2. **Art** — image generator produces hero/scene assets into
   `assets/`. Order: Gemini AI Studio "nano banana" (free, best
   quality/$0) → Pollinations.ai (free API, scriptable from tools/)
   → Ideogram (free, when art needs text) → paid (Higgsfield etc.)
   only if motion/video earns it.
3. **Assemble** — builder composes; mira critiques; jerry verifies.
   `design-to-motion` skill for animated showcase loops (MP4/GIF).
4. **Established sites keep their signature** — nightwright.dev's
   live sky stays; generated art is for pages with no identity yet.

Cost note: pipeline is $0 until art volume or video forces a paid
generator. Synced 2026-10-26.
